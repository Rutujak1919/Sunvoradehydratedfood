Our Story

Sunvora Foods is a trusted manufacturer and supplier of premium dehydrated vegetables, flakes and natural herbal powders — Onion, Garlic, Ginger, Beetroot, Moringa, Amla, Turmeric and more. Every batch is sun-dried and stone-ground to preserve its natural taste, aroma, colour and nutrition, with no additives and no shortcuts, from our farms in Chikhali, Taluka Indapur, District Pune.
